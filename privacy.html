06. How Your Data Flows Through Our Systems

When you initiate a scan, your data passes through the following systems in sequence:

  1. Website scan submission form (scanmyshadow.com) — Initial data collection. User submits phone number and/or email address, along with their report delivery email, via a secure form on the Scan My Shadow website.
  2. Make.com (EU-hosted automation) — Orchestrates the scan pipeline. EU hosting selected for data governance compliance.
  3. Breach database scan tools — Automated query of publicly known breach databases and open digital footprint sources.
  4. PDFMonkey — PDF report generation. Data used solely to populate the report template.
  5. Email delivery — Final report delivered to the report delivery email provided by the user on the scan form.
  6. Maya (AI chat assistant) — Post-report support. Chat messages may be logged in anonymised form for service improvement purposes.
  7. Razorpay — Payment processing. Financial data does not pass through Scan My Shadow systems.
  8. Zoho Invoice — GST-compliant invoice generation. Transaction records retained 6 years per statute.
  9. Google Sheets — Internal operations and employee accountability logging. Not accessible to users.

All third-party processors are bound by their own data protection obligations. We do not share your personal data with any third party beyond what is strictly necessary for service delivery.

08. Data Retention

We operate a strict, legally compliant, time-limited data retention policy:

  • Scan data (the phone number and email address you submit for scanning): Permanently deleted immediately after your report is delivered, and cannot be recovered or produced thereafter.
  • Invoice record (name, phone number, payment amount, GST reference, generated by our invoicing system at the time of payment): Retained for 6 years as required under GST law and may be disclosed to law enforcement upon receipt of a valid legal order.
  • Chat messages (messages sent to Maya after report delivery): Personally identifiable context deleted immediately. Questions may be retained in anonymised form for service improvement purposes.

The phone number and email address you submit for scanning are deleted by design immediately after your report is delivered — not as a courtesy, but as a firm policy. They cannot be recovered or produced after that point, including in response to legal requests. Separately, our invoicing system generates and retains an invoice record (name, phone number, payment amount) for 6 years as required by GST law. This invoice record is a distinct system from your scan data, and is the only place a phone number is retained.

09. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — Request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — Request correction of inaccurate personal data we hold.
  • Right to erasure — Request deletion of your data, subject to statutory retention obligations.
  • Right to grievance redressal — Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (Clause 16).
  • Right to nominate — Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of incapacity or death.
